A Mission With Momentum

Nevada's aerospace future starts here

UNLV ATLAS-1 is a student-led CubeSat research project building Nevada's first satellite. We have one goal: make aerospace accessible in Nevada. We hope to expand opportunities in Nevada, creating pathways for aerospace education, research, and workforce development across Nevada.

Want to help? Get involved or become a sponsor.

Build. Validate. Fly. Repeat.

An Incremental Path to Orbit

Beginning with the MOSS kit, UNLV ATLAS-1 progresses from a ground-based satellite system to a high altitude balloon mission and ultimately to orbit. By increasing the fidelity of each system over time, this approach lowers the cost barrier to entry and lets team build CubeSat skills at an accessible level. As experience grows, teams can advance to systems with greater fidelity, cost, and complexity.

HAB Flight Campaign

FINALIZING

Refine communications, downlink environmental data, improve reliability, and increase system fidelity while developing skills needed for orbital satellite development, testing, and launch.

Orbital Preparation

BEGINNING

Transition to flight-qualified hardware, including development of the orbital payload. Complete ground-station development and satellite fabrication, then proceed with environmental testing, certification, launch-provider selection, and launch integration.

Phase 4 | PROSPECTIVE 2027

Orbital Mission

TARGET

Deploy in Low Earth Orbit, collect temperature and radiation data, and compare results with thermal models. Release all findings publicly as an open-source reference.

Our payload and research

Payload

Turn flight data into better engineering. UNLV ATLAS-1 is designed to measure temperature and radiation in Low Earth orbit. Distributed sensors will reveal thermal gradients across the structure, allowing the team to compare real conditions with analytical heat-transfer models and validate thermal performance predictions.

Research Areas

Data from UNLV ATLAS-1 supports research and student projects across:

  • Aerospace engineering and mission operations
  • Avionics and communications
  • Atmospheric and space environmental characterization
  • STEM education and open-source awareness

Build Like Us: MOSSKIT for Students

MOSSKIT is an open-source high-altitude balloon launch kit designed for students and teams wanting to learn space systems engineering. It's the foundation of UNLV ATLAS-1's approach—starting with accessible, ground-based hardware that teaches real mission operations skills before advancing to higher-fidelity systems.

Whether you're building your own satellite, learning about avionics, or exploring space systems, MOSSKIT gives you the tools to get started. All hardware and software are open-source and documented for your next mission.
